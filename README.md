# Desk Fitness App UX Research
**Decode Labs Internship | UI/UX Design Track - Project 1: The Empathy Map**

## 📌 Project Overview
A new fitness startup wants to launch an app for busy office professionals who struggle to find time for exercise. Before designing a single pixel, this research focuses deeply on understanding the "User's Pain" to design an ultra-accessible desk fitness companion that helps relieve physical strain and build consistent health habits via 5-minute micro-workouts right at their workstation.

## 👥 Target User Persona
* **Name:** Sarah, 32 [1]
* **Role:** Remote Software Engineer [1]
* **Behavioral Traits:** Works 45+ hours per week, sits at a desk 10 hours a day, and has low physical activity per day.

---

## 🧠 The 4-Quadrant Empathy Map
Derived from user insights to map out the real psychological and structural barriers faced by busy professionals during their workdays.

| Quadrant | User Experience Observations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🗣️ SAYS** | • "I'll absolutely start stretching and working out this coming Monday." <br> • "If this meeting doesn't wrap up in five minutes, I'm going to start responding in code." [2] |
| **💭 THINKS** | • "I am so tired, I want to lie down." <br> • "My life outside of this building is completely nonexistent right now." [2] |
| **🏃 DOES** | • Sits continuously for 10 hours a day with no walks and breaks. <br> • Drinks strong coffee to avoid afternoon energy slumps. <br> • Takes a five-minute desk-break after getting a soft ping from an app. |
| **❤️ FEELS** | • Overwhelmed by complex fitness apps demanding a one-hour gym commitment. <br> • Guilty when looking at a tracker for daily steps. <br> • Motivated by gamified micro-habits that reward standing and moving for two minutes. |

---

## ⚠️ Pains & Gains Analysis

### ❌ User Pains
* **Time Poverty:** Back-to-back meetings leave zero open calendar slots for long workouts.
* **Mental Exhaustion:** Too drained by 6:00 PM to make decisions about exercise routines.
* **High Friction:** Standard fitness apps require changing clothes, gym travel, or heavy equipment.
* **Screen Lock:** Becoming so deeply focused on work that hours pass without standing up.

###  User Gains
* **Desk Pain Relief:** Alleviating neck strain and lower back stiffness directly at their workstation.
* **Micro-Habit Success:** Building a consistent routine through effortless 5-to-10 minute movement windows.
* **Sustained Energy:** Fighting off the afternoon brain fog and energy slumps without relying on extra coffee.
* **Guilt-Free Workdays:** Feeling healthy and active without having to sacrifice work productivity or personal downtime.
